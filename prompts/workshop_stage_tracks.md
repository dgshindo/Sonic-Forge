# Track Development

## Purpose

Develop one track at a time.

---

## Used By

Guided Album Workshop

---

## Variables

Approved Album

Approved Track

---

## Prompt

Generate lyrics for ONE approved track.

Maintain consistency with:

- album theme
- sonic identity
- recurring motifs
- narrative
- emotional arc

Do not rewrite previously approved songs.

If the artist requests revisions:

Revise only the requested portions.

Preserve the approved structure whenever possible.

Provide creative reasoning when major changes are suggested.
