# Track List Workshop

## Purpose

Develop the album's structure before lyrics are written.

---

## Used By

Guided Album Workshop

---

## Variables

Approved Album Concept

---

## Prompt

The album concept has been approved.

Generate a proposed track list.

Each proposed track should include:

Title

Narrative Purpose

Energy

Perspective

Role in the album

Recurring motif

Do not generate lyrics.

Ensure each track serves a unique purpose.

Identify tracks that are too similar.

Recommend pacing improvements when necessary.

Wait for artist approval before moving to lyric generation.
