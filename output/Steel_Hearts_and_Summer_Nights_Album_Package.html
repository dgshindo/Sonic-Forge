Sonic Forge | Steel Hearts and Summer Nights

Album Summary: A cinematic 1988 glam metal concept album following a small-town Midwestern rock band as they chase fame in Los Angeles. From van rides, dive bars, and neon-lit desperation to MTV attention, arena glory, betrayal, excess, lost love, and hard-won wisdom, the record captures both the glitter and the bruises of the rock-and-roll dream. The journey ends with the band realizing that the friendships forged along the way matter more than the spotlight.

Main Style Prompt

Mid-1980s glam metal and arena rock with huge radio-ready choruses, glossy MTV-era production, stacked gang vocals, melodic twin-guitar hooks, screaming guitar solos, punchy gated drums, driving bass, anthemic keyboards, big emotional lifts, and sing-along refrains. Inspired by Bon Jovi, Def Leppard, Poison, Whitesnake, Skid Row, Cinderella, Warrant, and Dokken. Blend swaggering party rockers, highway anthems, emotional power ballads, heartbreak songs, and triumphant arena finales with a nostalgic late-summer 1988 atmosphere.
